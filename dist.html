Distributions - six more ways

Box plot, histogram, sorted curve and threshold strips were shown on 18/08. These are new.

1Ridgeline

Overlapping density curves. Rust tick is the worst 10% line.

2Violin

Mirrored density. White line median, rust line worst 10%.

3Decile dots

Nine dots per test. Where they bunch, companies bunch.

4Cumulative

Percentile on the x axis, the figure on the y. Read off any level.

5Matrix

One column per company, one row per test. Grey means not applicable.

No picture. The actual figure at each cut-off.
