What this does not do

  • The screen has not been backtested. Nobody knows whether these flags predict anything, including me.
  • Restatements, auditor changes and late filings come from SEC filings, so they are United States only. The 0 Australian names cannot carry them.
  • Australian operating cash flow is derived, because the published cash flow statements do not carry it directly. Where two independent derivations disagree, the accruals flag is dropped for that company rather than guessed.
  • Stock on hand is measured against revenue rather than cost of sales, because cost of sales is not reliably tagged. The ratio therefore moves with gross margin as well as with inventory.
  • A sixth test, on capitalised costs, was dropped: fewer than one company in ten reports the figure it needs.
  • Companies with fewer than three applicable flags are listed separately rather than ranked. There are 16 of them.
  • Banks, insurers and utilities often do not report a revenue line in the standard form, so some tests do not apply to them. Not applicable is shown as not applicable, never as a pass.

Sources: SEC EDGAR XBRL company facts and filing metadata for the United States; Yahoo Finance for Australia. Figures are as filed. This is not investment advice and not a recommendation about any company.
