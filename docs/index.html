Limits

  • The tests have not been backtested. Whether they predict anything is unknown.
  • The 18 companies that restated their accounts or changed auditor score no worse than average here. Short interest identified them; these tests did not.
  • Restatements and auditor changes come from SEC filings, so the 197 Australian companies have none.
  • Australian filings do not report operating cash flow, so it is worked back two ways. Where the two disagree, the accruals test is dropped for that company.
  • Inventory is measured against revenue rather than cost of sales, which is not reliably tagged.
  • 41 companies have fewer than three tests that apply, so they are not ranked. A test that does not apply is not a pass.

SEC EDGAR for the United States, Yahoo Finance for Australia, as filed. Not investment advice.
